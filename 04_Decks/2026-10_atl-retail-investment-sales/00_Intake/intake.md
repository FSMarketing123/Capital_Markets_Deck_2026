# Capital Markets Intake Questionnaire

For investment sales and debt & equity pitches to owners and sellers. Answer these
**before** building. Every answer maps to a section of the Capital Markets template, in
page order.

- Leave a question blank to keep the template's version.
- Write `REMOVE` if that element should come out of this deck.
- Write `TBD` for anything unknown — the QC check flags any `TBD` left on the site.
- Drop files into the matching deck folder and write the filename here.

> Tip: paste this file to Claude and say *"Interview me through this intake one section at a
> time"* (Prompt 1 in `03_Claude-Prompts.md`).
