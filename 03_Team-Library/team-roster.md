## Bios (from the PDF)

### Bryan Belk

As a specialist in retail properties across the Southeast, Bryan Belk, Managing Director, provides maximized investment proceeds to his clients by preparing and executing detailed marketing plans, each tailored to the property and investor. Using his national database of more than 100,000 buyers and brokers, Mr. Belk and his team have helped institutions, developers and individuals achieve top market pricing while consecutively completing seamless transactions. Aligned with Franklin Street’s retail investment sales team, he has helped transact more than $2.6 billion for commercial real estate clients across the Southeast. Since starting his career, Mr. Belk has assisted the team in the closings of more than 700 properties. Active in the commercial real estate community, Mr. Belk has received several awards including Franklin Street’s 2010-2026 Sales Achievement Award, the 2012 and 2014 award winner of the highly regarded Franklin Street Award, and multiple years of being the Top Producer in the Atlanta Office. He is a member of the International Council of Shopping Centers (ICSC), Young Atlanta Retail Alliance (YARA), and the Association of Young Real Estate Professionals (AYREP). Mr. Belk was also recently awarded Top 50 under 40 and Top Retail Influencer by GlobeSt Real Estate Forum.

### John Tennant

With more than 35 years of commercial real estate expertise throughout the U.S., John Tennant, Managing Director with Franklin Street, specializes in the sale of single-tenant and retail properties, helping clients achieve maximum value for the sale of their assets. Mr. Tennant joined Franklin Street in 2010 as a Senior Director and a key contributor to the Franklin Street investment sales team. He has been involved in more than 10 million square feet of leasing and development during his real estate career and his background in leasing, coupled with his development expertise, gives him a unique perspective for each of his clients. Prior to joining Franklin Street, Mr. Tennant was a Development Partner with Hendon Properties LLC in Atlanta for six years where he was responsible for all aspects of development and leasing. Mr. Tennant began his career in retail commercial real estate in 1987 with New Market/Cousins Properties and handled outparcel and shop leasing activity for its Northeast power center portfolio. As a leader in the real estate community, Mr. Tennant has been the recipient of many industry awards including Franklin Street’s 2012- 2025 Sales Achievement Award, CoStar’s 2012 Power Broker award, and the 2007 Fulton County New Development Outlook Award. He is also a member of the International Council of Shopping Centers (ICSC) and the Georgia Chamber of Commerce.

Prior to joining Franklin Street, Brian built a reputation for identifying high-growth retail corridors and structuring value-driven opportunities for both tenants and property owners. His background in tenant representation and development gives him a unique perspective when advising clients on acquisitions, dispositions, and repositioning strategies within today’s evolving retail landscape. Brian’s expertise includes single-tenant net lease investments, QSR and drive-thru development, shopping center leasing, retail redevelopment, and site selection strategy. He is known for his relationship-driven approach, market knowledge, and ability to connect retailers, developers, and investors to create successful long-term outcomes.
